Safety first: start at low amplification and increase slowly. The output limiter helps prevent sudden loud peaks, but this is not a medical hearing aid.

Background use: install the app, keep the screen awake, and exclude it from battery optimization if your phone allows it. Browsers and mobile operating systems may still suspend microphone processing when the app is fully backgrounded; a web app cannot guarantee continuous audio after that.
